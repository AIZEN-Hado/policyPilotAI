# Structural reference register

Reviewed 2026-11-07. These are public primary-source references, not real claim records.
They inform the structure of the fictional documents, not policy eligibility decisions.

| Source | Observed structural feature | Use and limits |
|---|---|---|
| [HDFC ERGO public health claim form](https://www.hdfcergo.com/documents/downloads/claimforms/HEALTHSURAKSHA-CLAIMFORM.pdf) | Insured/member identification, hospitalization dates, expense categories, attachment checklist | Field grouping informed original fixture form; older public form, not asserted to be current universal procedure. No logo, signatures or wording copied. |
| [HDFC ERGO document checklist](https://www.hdfcergo.com/claim/register-health-insurance-claim/document-check-list) | Discharge record, itemized final bill, payment evidence and supporting investigation documents | Informed bundle composition. Insurer-specific procedural requirements are not encoded as universal rules. |
| [HDFC ERGO policy wording directory](https://www.hdfcergo.com/download/policy-wordings/health) | Product identifier and coverage start date select wording version | Informed explicit schedule/product/version linkage. SYN identifiers are not genuine UINs. |

The source register does not establish clinical plausibility, representative hospital
pricing or national document distributions. No hospital-specific billing template has
been independently verified. Hospital layouts and all policy terms are original
fictional test designs. A domain reviewer and native-language reviewer are still needed
before real-world claims about clinical or multilingual accuracy.
